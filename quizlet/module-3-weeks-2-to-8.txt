The two layers of the trophoblast	Cytotrophoblast (inner) and syncytiotrophoblast (outer)
Cytotrophoblast	Inner layer of mononucleated cells
Syncytiotrophoblast	Outer multinucleated zone without distinct cell boundaries; erodes maternal tissue and sinusoids, and secretes hCG
Which layer secretes hCG?	The syncytiotrophoblast
Which layer establishes primitive uteroplacental circulation?	The syncytiotrophoblast, by eroding maternal tissue and sinusoids
Bilaminar disc	Epiblast plus hypoblast, formed from the embryoblast in week 2
Epiblast	High columnar cells adjacent to the amniotic cavity; gives rise to ALL three primary germ layers
Hypoblast	Small cuboidal cells adjacent to the blastocyst cavity and yolk sac
Which layer gives rise to all 3 germ layers?	The epiblast
Gastrulation	Week 3: establishes all three germ layers by invagination through the primitive streak on the epiblast
The three germ layers	Ectoderm, mesoderm, endoderm
EXAM TRAP: failure of the primitive streak to degenerate	Sacrococcygeal teratoma
Sacrococcygeal teratoma is caused by	Failure of the primitive streak to degenerate
Notochord	Midline cellular rod that provides structural support and induces the overlying ectoderm to undergo neurulation
Neurulation sequence	Neural plate, then neural groove, then neural tube
What does the neural tube form?	The central nervous system: brain and spinal cord
Neural crest cells	Detach during neural fold closure and migrate to form the PNS (sensory and autonomic ganglia), melanocytes, Schwann cells and the adrenal medulla
Ectoderm derivatives	Nervous system (CNS and PNS), epidermis of skin, hair, nails, sweat glands, adrenal medulla
Mesoderm derivatives	Bones, cartilage, connective tissue, muscle (skeletal, cardiac, smooth), cardiovascular system, kidneys, gonads
Endoderm derivatives	Epithelial lining of the GI tract, respiratory tract (trachea, lungs), liver, pancreas, urinary bladder
Paraxial mesoderm	Segments into somites: about 3 pairs per day, 42 to 44 pairs in total
How many somite pairs form?	42 to 44 pairs, at about 3 pairs per day
The three parts of a somite	Sclerotome, myotome, dermatome
Sclerotome	Bones and cartilage of the vertebral column
Myotome	Skeletal muscle
Dermatome	Dermis of the skin
Intermediate mesoderm	Forms the urogenital system: kidneys and gonads
Lateral plate mesoderm	Splits into a splanchnic (visceral) layer and a somatic (parietal) layer
