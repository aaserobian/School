Trunk	The torso: the central axis of the body
Boundaries of the trunk	Inferior to the neck, superior to the lower limbs, and between the upper limbs
The 5 major subdivisions of the trunk	Thorax, Abdomen, Pelvis, Perineum, Back (dorsal trunk)
Thorax	The chest cavity: ribs, sternum, heart, lungs
Abdomen	The region between thorax and pelvis: the digestive tract
Pelvis	Inferior region within the pelvic girdle: bladder, rectum, reproductive organs
Perineum	Diamond-shaped region on the inferior pelvic floor: external genitalia and anus
Back (dorsal trunk)	Posterior region housing the vertebral column and spinal muscles
Anterior surface landmarks of the trunk	Sternal, pectoral, mammary, umbilical, inguinal
Posterior surface landmarks of the trunk	Vertebral, scapular, lumbar, sacral, gluteal
Sternal	Breastbone (anterior)
Pectoral	Chest (anterior)
Mammary	Breast (anterior)
Umbilical	Navel (anterior)
Inguinal	Groin (anterior)
Vertebral	Spine (posterior)
Scapular	Shoulder blade (posterior)
Lumbar	Lower back (posterior)
Sacral	Between the hips (posterior)
Gluteal	Buttock (posterior)
Superior vs. Inferior	Superior = towards the head; Inferior = towards the feet
Anterior (ventral) vs. Posterior (dorsal)	Anterior = front; Posterior = back
Medial vs. Lateral	Medial = closer to the midline; Lateral = further from the midline
Ipsilateral vs. Contralateral	Ipsilateral = same side of the body; Contralateral = opposite sides
Superficial vs. Deep	Superficial = closer to the skin surface; Deep = further inward
Parietal vs. Visceral	Parietal = lining the wall of a body cavity; Visceral = directly covering an internal organ
Median (midsagittal) plane	Vertical plane dividing the trunk into equal right and left halves
Sagittal plane	Vertical plane parallel to the median, dividing into unequal right and left parts
Coronal (frontal) plane	Vertical plane dividing into anterior and posterior parts
Transverse (axial) plane	Horizontal plane dividing into superior and inferior parts
What does the diaphragm separate?	The thoracic cavity from the abdominopelvic cavity
What does the thoracic cavity contain?	The pleural and pericardial cavities
