Blastomeres | The individual daughter cells formed during the rapid mitotic divisions (cleavage) of the zygote. They get progressively smaller with each division; at the 16-cell stage (Day 3) compacted blastomeres form the solid sphere called the morula.
Blastocyst | The fluid-filled hollow sphere that forms around Day 4-5 as the morula enters the uterine cavity. Consists of a central cavity (blastocoele), an inner cell mass (embryoblast) and an outer cell wall (trophoblast). Begins implantation at the end of Week 1 (Day 6).
Embryoblast (inner cell mass) | The cluster of cells located at one pole inside the blastocyst. Gives rise to the embryo proper, differentiating into the bilaminar disc in Week 2.
Trophoblast (outer cell mass) | The outer epithelial wall surrounding the blastocyst cavity. Forms the fetal contribution to the placenta and the outer embryonic membranes; differentiates into two distinct layers during Week 2.
Cytotrophoblast | The INNER cellular layer of the trophoblast, consisting of distinct mononucleated cells. Actively undergoes mitosis; proliferating cells form cellular columns (primary villi) that penetrate outward into the syncytium.
Syncytiotrophoblast | The OUTER multinucleated mass of the trophoblast, without distinct individual cell boundaries. Highly invasive - erodes maternal endometrial vessels and sinusoids to establish primitive uteroplacental circulation. Secretes hCG (maintains the corpus luteum; detected by pregnancy tests), Early Pregnancy Factor (EPF) and Tumor Necrosis Factor (TNF).
Epiblast | The DORSAL layer of high COLUMNAR cells in the bilaminar germ disc, adjacent to the amniotic cavity. Origin of ALL THREE primary germ layers (ectoderm, mesoderm, endoderm) via invagination during gastrulation.
Hypoblast | The VENTRAL layer of small CUBOIDAL cells in the bilaminar germ disc, adjacent to the blastocyst cavity. Contributes to the lining of the yolk sac (Heuser's membrane) and is displaced by migrating epiblast cells during gastrulation to help form endoderm.
Amnioblasts | Specialised cells that arise directly from the epiblast. They line the amniotic cavity, superior to the epiblast layer.
Myoblasts | Embryonic progenitor cells derived from the myotome division of the somites (paraxial mesoderm). Spindle-shaped cells that align and fuse end-to-end to form multinucleated myotubes, which mature into striated skeletal muscle fibres.
