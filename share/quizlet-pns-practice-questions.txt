How many pairs of spinal nerves are present in the human body? A. 30 / B. 31 / C. 32 / D. 33	B. 31 - There are 31 pairs: 8 cervical, 12 thoracic, 5 lumbar, 5 sacral, and 1 coccygeal.
Which cells are responsible for myelination in the PNS? A. Oligodendrocytes / B. Astrocytes / C. Schwann cells / D. Microglia	C. Schwann cells - Schwann cells form the myelin sheath around peripheral nerve fibers.
Which type of fibers carry motor signals to skeletal muscles? A. GSA / B. GSE / C. GVA / D. GVE	B. GSE - General Somatic Efferent (GSE) fibers innervate skeletal muscles.
What is the function of general visceral afferent (GVA) fibers? A. Send motor signals to glands / B. Receive sensory input from visceral organs / C. Transmit pain from skin / D. Control skeletal muscle movement	B. Receive sensory input from visceral organs - GVA fibers carry sensory information from internal organs.
Which plexus supplies the upper limb? A. Cervical / B. Brachial / C. Lumbar / D. Sacral	B. Brachial - The brachial plexus (C5-T1) innervates the upper limb.
Which division of the ANS is responsible for 'fight or flight'? A. Somatic / B. Parasympathetic / C. Sympathetic / D. Central	C. Sympathetic - The sympathetic division activates stress responses.
Which dermatome corresponds to the umbilicus? A. T4 / B. T10 / C. L1 / D. S1	B. T10 - T10 dermatome is at the level of the umbilicus.
Which ganglia are associated with the sympathetic nervous system? A. Cranial / B. Intramural / C. Paravertebral / D. None	C. Paravertebral - Paravertebral ganglia form the sympathetic chain.
Which plexus is formed by L1-L4 spinal nerves? A. Cervical / B. Brachial / C. Lumbar / D. Sacral	C. Lumbar - The lumbar plexus arises from L1-L4.
Which fibers transmit pain and proprioception from the skin? A. GSE / B. GVA / C. GSA / D. SSA	C. GSA - GSA fibers carry somatic sensory stimuli.
What type of fibers innervate smooth muscle and glands? A. GSE / B. GVE / C. SSA / D. SVE	B. GVE - General Visceral Efferent (GVE) fibers target autonomic effectors.
Which spinal nerves form the sacral plexus? A. L1-L4 / B. L4-S4 / C. C5-T1 / D. T1-T12	B. L4-S4 - Sacral plexus includes L4-S4.
Which structure contains sensory neuron cell bodies? A. Ventral root / B. Dorsal root ganglion / C. Gray matter / D. White matter	B. Dorsal root ganglion - Dorsal root ganglia house sensory neuron cell bodies.
Which type of neuron conducts impulses from CNS to effectors? A. Sensory / B. Interneuron / C. Motor / D. Association	C. Motor - Motor neurons carry signals from CNS to muscles/glands.
Which part of the spinal nerve contains both sensory and motor fibers? A. Dorsal root / B. Ventral root / C. Spinal nerve / D. Dorsal root ganglion	C. Spinal nerve - Spinal nerves are mixed nerves.
Which division of the ANS conserves energy? A. Sympathetic / B. Parasympathetic / C. Somatic / D. Central	B. Parasympathetic - Parasympathetic division promotes rest and digestion.
What is a dermatome? A. A nerve plexus / B. A spinal ganglion / C. An area of skin supplied by a single spinal nerve / D. A type of motor fiber	C. An area of skin supplied by a single spinal nerve - Dermatomes map skin regions to spinal nerves.
Which spinal nerve region has 12 pairs? A. Cervical / B. Thoracic / C. Lumbar / D. Sacral	B. Thoracic - The thoracic region has 12 pairs of spinal nerves.
Which type of fiber is involved in autonomic motor control? A. GSE / B. GVE / C. SSA / D. SVA	B. GVE - GVE fibers innervate smooth muscle, cardiac muscle, and glands.
Which spinal nerve region has 8 pairs? A. Thoracic / B. Lumbar / C. Cervical / D. Sacral	C. Cervical - There are 8 cervical spinal nerves (C1-C8).
Which structure carries motor fibers from the spinal cord? A. Dorsal root / B. Ventral root / C. Dorsal root ganglion / D. Spinal nerve	B. Ventral root - Ventral roots carry motor (efferent) fibers.
Which autonomic division has craniosacral origin? A. Sympathetic / B. Parasympathetic / C. Somatic / D. Central	B. Parasympathetic - Parasympathetic fibers originate from the brainstem and S2-S4.
Which autonomic division has thoracolumbar origin? A. Sympathetic / B. Parasympathetic / C. Somatic / D. Central	A. Sympathetic - Sympathetic fibers arise from T1-L2 spinal segments.
Which ganglia are located near or within target organs? A. Paravertebral / B. Prevertebral / C. Intramural / D. Dorsal root	C. Intramural - Intramural ganglia are found in or near the target organs (parasympathetic).
Which fibers carry sensory input from joints and tendons? A. GSA / B. GVA / C. SSA / D. SVA	A. GSA - GSA fibers transmit somatic sensory input including proprioception.
Which autonomic division promotes digestion and glandular secretion? A. Sympathetic / B. Parasympathetic / C. Somatic / D. Central	B. Parasympathetic - Parasympathetic division supports rest and digest functions.
Which spinal nerve region contributes to the brachial plexus? A. C5-T1 / B. T1-T12 / C. L1-L4 / D. S1-S4	A. C5-T1 - The brachial plexus is formed by C5-T1.
Which structure is the site of synapse for autonomic motor neurons? A. Dorsal horn / B. Ventral horn / C. Autonomic ganglion / D. Spinal nerve	C. Autonomic ganglion - Autonomic ganglia are where pre- and postganglionic neurons synapse.
Which spinal nerves form the cervical plexus? A. C1-C4 / B. C5-T1 / C. T1-T12 / D. L1-L4	A. C1-C4 - The cervical plexus includes C1-C4.
Which spinal nerves form the lumbar plexus? A. C1-C4 / B. C5-T1 / C. L1-L4 / D. S1-S4	C. L1-L4 - Lumbar plexus arises from L1-L4.
Which structure contains motor neuron cell bodies? A. Dorsal horn / B. Ventral horn / C. Dorsal root ganglion / D. White matter	B. Ventral horn - Ventral horn of the spinal cord contains motor neuron cell bodies.
Which autonomic division is energy-expending? A. Parasympathetic / B. Sympathetic / C. Somatic / D. Central	B. Sympathetic - Sympathetic division prepares the body for action.
Which autonomic division regulates glandular secretion? A. Somatic / B. Sympathetic / C. Parasympathetic / D. Both B and C	D. Both B and C - Both sympathetic and parasympathetic divisions regulate glands.
Where are the cell bodies of sympathetic preganglionic neurons located? A. Brainstem / B. Thoracolumbar spinal cord (T1-L2) / C. Sacral spinal cord (S2-S4) / D. Paravertebral ganglia	B. Thoracolumbar spinal cord (T1-L2) - Sympathetic preganglionic neurons originate in the lateral horn of the thoracolumbar spinal cord (T1-L2).
Where are the cell bodies of parasympathetic preganglionic neurons located? A. Thoracolumbar spinal cord / B. Brainstem and sacral spinal cord (S2-S4) / C. Paravertebral ganglia / D. Prevertebral ganglia	B. Brainstem and sacral spinal cord (S2-S4) - Parasympathetic preganglionic neurons arise from cranial nerve nuclei in the brainstem and the sacral spinal cord (S2-S4).
Where are sympathetic postganglionic neurons typically located? A. Intramural ganglia / B. Cranial ganglia / C. Paravertebral and prevertebral ganglia / D. Dorsal root ganglia	C. Paravertebral and prevertebral ganglia - Sympathetic postganglionic neurons are found in paravertebral (sympathetic chain) and prevertebral ganglia.
Where are parasympathetic postganglionic neurons typically located? A. Near or within the target organ (intramural ganglia) / B. Paravertebral ganglia / C. Prevertebral ganglia / D. Thoracolumbar spinal cord	A. Near or within the target organ (intramural ganglia) - Parasympathetic postganglionic neurons are located in ganglia close to or within the walls of the target organs.
Which autonomic division has long preganglionic and short postganglionic fibers? A. Sympathetic / B. Parasympathetic / C. Somatic / D. Central	B. Parasympathetic - Parasympathetic pathways feature long preganglionic fibers that synapse in ganglia near the target organ.
Where are the cell bodies of the first-order sensory neurons located? A. Ventral horn of the spinal cord / B. Dorsal root ganglion / C. Paravertebral ganglion / D. Brainstem nuclei	B. Dorsal root ganglion - The first-order sensory neurons have their cell bodies in the dorsal root ganglia, located just outside the spinal cord.
Where are the cell bodies of somatic motor neurons located in the spinal cord? A. Dorsal horn / B. Dorsal root ganglion / C. Ventral horn / D. Lateral horn	C. Ventral horn - Somatic motor neurons originate in the ventral horn of the spinal cord and send axons through the ventral root.
Which part of the spinal cord contains sensory neuron synapses? A. Ventral horn / B. Dorsal horn / C. Lateral horn / D. Central canal	B. Dorsal horn - Sensory neurons synapse in the dorsal horn of the spinal cord gray matter.
Where are autonomic (visceral) motor neuron cell bodies located in the spinal cord? A. Dorsal horn / B. Ventral horn / C. Lateral horn / D. Dorsal root ganglion	C. Lateral horn - Autonomic motor neurons (preganglionic) originate in the lateral horn of the spinal cord (T1-L2 for sympathetic, S2-S4 for parasympathetic).
